## What we are planning to build

Underdawg becomes the business side of Instagram for artists. Creators connect Instagram, and there's no separate feed or second account.

**App layout**
- Bottom tabs: Home · Page · + · Club · You
- Art Shop and Gigs sit under Home → Ways to earn

**Home (creator dashboard)**
- Earnings today and this month, with a Withdraw button
- "Needs you" to-do list: session starting soon, work to review, brand requests, new bookings, club replies
- Next session with seats sold, and Share / Start buttons
- DM automation results today: comments → DMs → sales
- Instagram snapshot: new followers, engagement rate, best time to post
- Page visits and top link
- Club activity
- Ways to earn: Art Shop, Sessions, Bookings, Downloads, Tips, Fan Club, Hire requests, Gigs
- Setup checklist for new creators, so Home is never empty

**Instagram profile**
- Login with Instagram or Google
- Posts, followers and audience synced from Instagram
- Engagement rate: normal and "True ER" (verified)
- Growth, top posts, best time to post
- Suggested rate for brand work
- Schedule posts to Instagram

**Link-in-bio page (`underdawg.com/username`)**
- Links, Instagram grid, stats
- Book me, Hire me, Shop, Tip
- Classes, tickets, free downloads
- Email / WhatsApp signup
- Reviews
- YouTube / Spotify embeds
- Fully editable and customisable
- Templates: basic ones free, more on the paid plan
- "Match my Instagram" colours
- Separate views for fans and for brands
- Money earned per link
- UPI payments on the page

**Instagram DM automation**
- Any trigger word the creator chooses
- Works on comments, story replies, DMs, Live comments
- Sends messages, links, images, files, buttons
- Sends bookings, payments, portfolio, tickets
- Collects fan email / phone
- Public reply to the comment
- Follower check, send once per person
- Ready-made templates

**Art Shop (sell any art) & AI mockups**
- Sell any art you make: original paintings, limited or open prints, photo prints, handmade pieces, crafts
- List an item: photos, type, size, medium, price, quantity ("1 of 1" for originals), dispatch time
- Ship it yourself: the buyer pays, the artist books a courier pickup or adds a tracking number
- Shipping price by zone, or local pickup
- Optional print partner for prints, tees and mugs (no stock, no upfront cost)
- AI mockups: the artwork shown on a wall in a room, or on a product
- Certificate of authenticity for originals
- Buyer protection: tracking required, damage and return rules
- Sold on the link-in-bio page and through Instagram DMs
- Orders and sales tracking

**Live Rooms (Discord-style sessions)**
- Creator sets up a session: title, date, length, price, seats
- Paid or free
- Fans pay by UPI and join
- Room with live video, chat, raise hand, pinned notes, tips
- Room closes when the session ends and everyone is removed
- Automatic refund if the creator cancels
- Rating and "book the next class" after the session
- Replay for 48 hours (paid sessions)
- Free sessions: up to 45 minutes, 30 people, 2 per month
- Sell sessions through Instagram DMs and the link-in-bio page

**Fan Club (in-app community, Reddit-style)**
- Each creator has their own Fan Club inside the app
- Free members plus paid tiers (e.g. ₹49 / ₹199 a month)
- Creator posts: updates, behind the scenes, early drops, members-only posts
- Fan posts: fan art, questions, covers, photos in merch
- Upvotes, comments and replies; sort by Hot / New / Top
- Polls ("Which design should I print next?")
- **Show & Review:**
  - Fans post their work: a drawing, a dance video, a singing cover, a photo.
  - The artist rates it (stars) and says what to fix, by text or voice note. Later: draw on the image or comment at a moment in the video.
  - Fans fix it and post again, with a before/after view.
  - Paid members get their work reviewed first. Others can pay for a review (e.g. ₹99).
  - The best work gets featured in the club.
- **Help posts:** fans ask about a problem ("My spin keeps failing"). The artist or other members answer, and the best answer is marked "Solved".
- Ratings and reviews of classes, merch and sessions, from verified buyers only
- Fan points and badges (Top Fan, OG Member) and a leaderboard
- Member perks: members-only Live Rooms, early merch access, discount codes
- Creator and chosen fans can moderate (pin, remove, ban); report and block buttons
- Fans can join from an Instagram DM or the link-in-bio page

**Artist Boards (Reddit-style, artists only, in the app, built on the same system as Fan Club)**
- Brand Reviews
- Scam Alerts
- Rate Check ("Is this offer fair?")
- Feedback on work
- Upvotes and comments

**Hire Artists (optional, for brands)**
- Artists choose whether to turn on "Open for work" (off by default)
- Brands sign up for free with a brand account
- Brands search artists by craft, city, budget, followers and engagement rate
- Hire directly from the artist's profile, or post a short brief and artists apply
- The artist can accept, counter-offer or decline
- The brand pays upfront, and the money is held until the work is approved
- Simple contract (click to accept), work upload, 2 revisions included
- Brand and artist rate each other after the job

**Gigs (open gig board)**
- Brands, venues, event organisers, cafés, colleges, wedding planners and individuals post paid gigs
- Gig types: live performances (singer, DJ, band, dancer, comedian), event art (live painting, caricature), freelance creative work (logo, illustration, video edit, photography)
- Each post shows craft, city, date and budget, from a verified poster
- Artists filter by craft, city, date and budget, and apply with their portfolio and a quote
- Artists can also be invited
- Advance payment is held until the gig is done, then released
- Both sides rate each other

**Ways to earn**
- Tips (UPI)
- Free and paid digital downloads
- Bookings and 1:1 sessions
- Paid live sessions and event tickets
- Fan Club memberships
- Art Shop sales (originals, prints, handmade pieces)
- Brand work through Hire Artists (money held until the work is approved)
- Paid gigs through the Gigs board
- Template marketplace

**Pro plan (₹299/month)**
- Unlimited DM automations
- Premium templates
- Full stats
- No Underdawg badge
- Custom domain
- Money per link
- Free live sessions in Underdawg rooms
