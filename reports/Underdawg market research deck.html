The big answer

The plan as written does not deliver discovery.

It is mostly a money and Instagram toolkit. It helps artists who already have fans.

Only 3 of 18 features put an unknown artist in front of new people: Hire Artists, Gigs and a rebuilt Show & Review.

This is the main finding. The tools pay out in proportion to the audience an artist already has.

What should change

Become the place where the right people find unknown artists

Keep

Instagram connection for import, the link page, Hire Artists and Gigs, Show & Review, Artist Boards, UPI payments on the web.

Change

Show & Review becomes artist and curator review. Insights become a verified credibility card. Pro sells tools, never reach.

Add

A free first-audience queue, verified curators, bookers and brands, an India-first opportunity board, a no-pay-for-reach rule.

Drop

Fan Club, scheduling, DM automation in the MVP, the template marketplace, in-house ticketing, any paid visibility.

Launch in one city with 3 to 5 crafts, partly run by hand.

Curators, bookers, brands and collaborators find artists ranked by fit. Nobody can pay to jump the queue.

Feature 7 · Art Shop

Art Shop: start with Shop-lite, where the artist ships and is paid directly

1. List

Type, at least 3 real photos, size, price, dispatch time, shipping or local pickup.

2. Preview

An optional AI wall mockup at true scale, labelled as a visualisation.

3. Sell

A Shop block on the link page, and a button in Instagram DMs.

4. Pay

Prepaid only, into the artist's own payment account. 0% fee in the experiment.

5. Ship

Packing checklist, then book a courier pickup or enter a tracking number.

6. Certify

On delivery the sale is verified. Originals get a digital certificate.

After product-market fit: a licensed payment aggregator holds the money and releases it 5 to 7 days after delivery. Fee about 8 to 10%.

If Underdawg holds buyers' money it becomes a GST e-commerce operator, and unregistered artists could then sell only inside their own state. Get a chartered accountant's opinion first.

Features 16 and 17 · Hire Artists and Gigs

One Opportunities marketplace, with two doors

1. Verified posters

A business check or KYC. Favour cafés, venues, colleges and agencies.

2. Budget shown

Craft, city, date and a mandatory budget. Paid work only.

3. Free to apply

Portfolio and a quote. A weekly cap stops spam.

4. Fair shortlist

Blind first round, newcomer slots, ranked by fit and city.

5. Contract

Click to accept: date, deliverables and cancellation terms.

6. Advance held

By an RBI-authorised payment aggregator, not by Underdawg.

7. Release

After the gig, or automatically after a short dispute window.

8. Ratings and fees

Two-way ratings. The poster pays; artists pay 0%.

Door 1: hire this artist or send a brief. Door 2: post a gig or open call. Start by hand in one city; add escrow once posters come back.

Demand exists but cannot find artists: 83% of Indian marketers struggle to find the right creators.

Failed products · Lessons

Six lessons from Indian products that failed

Do not fight for feed time

Moj, Josh and Chingari lost their users to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

India-first is not a moat

Koo raised $65M and shut in July 2024.

Fan-payment apps need fans first

Rigi pivoted away from paid communities. Qoohoo's revenue fell 95.5%.

Live teaching beats celebrity content

FrontRow shut; Artium earns ₹28 crore from live music classes.

Protect payouts first

Brands pay in 90 days to a year. Hold the poster's money before work starts.

Charge the demand side

Brands, hirers and organisers pay. Unknown artists pay 0%.

Also: never take AI-training rights over artists' work, and promise export and backups.

Strategy · What is missing

Eight missing pieces that would make discovery real

First-audience queue

Every new work is shown free to matched peers and one curator, booker or brand scout.

Newcomer bonus and caps

Published rules, so big accounts cannot dominate.

Curator review queue

With deadlines, and no pay-per-pitch for artists.

India-first opportunity board

Verified organisers and a free-to-apply filter.

Opportunity-giver search

By skill, style, city, language and budget.

Human-made evidence

Light proof of process, and a buyer filter.

No-pay-for-reach rule

Published on day one.

Portable portfolio

Export, plus fan contacts the artist owns.

Each one answers an artist problem the research found evidence for.

The first-audience queue is the discovery engine. It can start by hand at 1,000 artists.

The sentence: artists get discovery and opportunity through Underdawg that they do not get elsewhere.

Scope: one city, 3 to 5 crafts, invite-only. For example illustrators and photographers in Mumbai.

Strategy · Core user journey

From joining to a paid opportunity, in eight steps

1. Join

By invite. Connect Instagram or upload work by hand.

2. Profile

Curated work, the credibility card, the link page.

3. Upload

Send a work to the review queue, with a goal.

4. Discovery

Matched peers and one opportunity-giver see it, by a deadline.

5. Found

Through the queue, a weekly digest and search by fit.

6. Opportunity

A pick, a shortlist, a brief, a gig or an open-call selection.

7. Value

Feedback, a pick or a paid job, with verified completion.

8. Return

Alerts for briefs and gigs in their city and craft.

Measure: the share of artists who get a real opportunity within 90 days from someone they did not know.

Step 4 is the one Instagram and Linktree do not offer.

Strategy · Positioning and moat

Today there is no moat. Here is what could become one.

A network of opportunity-givers

Verified curators, bookers and brands in each city and craft. Slow to build, and impossible to copy as a feature.

Trust rules rivals cannot adopt

No pay-for-reach, no AI training on artists' work, no gated contact. Rivals earn from the opposite.

Data from before traction

Curator picks, critique scores, completed jobs, payments and Indian rates by craft and city.

The least-occupied position: fair discovery of under-discovered artists by the people who create opportunities, across crafts, India-first.

UPI and GST invoices are a convenience, not a moat.

The beta site says both 'The Creator OS' and 'Get Discovered'. The current plan only delivers the first.

Grow by proof: one city, then density, then national scale

  • Escrow through an aggregator

Year 3: go national

About 100,000 artists

  • National marketplace
  • Brand reputation scores
  • Rupee price suggestions
  • Talent-scouting dashboard
  • Pro as real revenue

Gate 1: posters come back and artists get work from strangers

Gate 2: enough artists per craft and city for self-serve hiring

Move to the next stage when the gate is met, not when the calendar says so. Artist numbers are estimates to test.

The real limit at every stage is the number of opportunity-givers per city and craft.

Next step

Test the riskiest assumption first

Will Indian brands, venues, colleges and curators hire unknown artists through Underdawg, again and again? No evidence was found either way.

  • Recruit repeat opportunity-givers by hand in one city
  • Ask for real briefs with real budgets, and run weekly shortlists by hand
  • Measure briefs posted, posters who return, and hires of small-account artists

Confidence is moderate: many Indian creator numbers are company claims, and nobody has measured artist income by craft or the share of creators paid late.

If opportunity-givers will not post and return when the service is free and hand-run, software will not fix it.
