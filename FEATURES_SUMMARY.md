# Underdawg — Features Summary

## Features we have now

The app is a demo: screens are built, but it runs on sample data. Login, the server and payments are not connected yet.

- **Onboarding and login:** welcome slides, phone/Google login screens, choose role and craft, set name and photo.
- **Home feed:** For You / Following / Rising posts with likes, comments and follows.
- **Profile:** profile page with bio, badges and stats; edit profile with photo crop.
- **Portfolio:** portfolio page with availability, niches and rates, plus a public preview.
- **Camera and posts:** take photos and videos; create image, video, text and audio posts.
- **Drafts and scheduling:** save posts as drafts and pick a time to post.
- **Messages:** chat list with filters, and chat with typing indicator.
- **Gigs:** job board, apply to jobs, track applications and deals, rate card.
- **Merch store builder:** 6 steps (name, theme, banners, layout, products, mockups), with designs placed on real product photos.
- **Live web store link:** publish the store and share it as a web page.
- **Finance:** balance, transactions, payouts, tax estimate, invoices.
- **Analytics:** overview, audience, content performance, earnings.
- **Audience:** connect social platforms, top fans, email list, landing page builder.
- **Community:** groups, events, mentorship, Q&A, polls, challenges.
- **Learning:** courses with lesson progress.
- **Art marketplace:** list artwork for sale, commissions.
- **Reputation:** score, tiers, badges, ID verification steps.
- **Tips:** tip settings and tip history.
- **Collab:** send and receive collab requests with a revenue split.
- **Notifications:** list of likes, comments, follows, gig updates.
- **Settings:** light/dark theme, privacy, notifications, account, security, help.

## Features we plan to add next

Underdawg becomes the business side of Instagram for artists. Creators connect Instagram, and there's no separate feed.

- **New app layout:** bottom tabs Home · Page · + · Club · You.
- **Home:** the creator's daily dashboard. It shows earnings, a "Needs you" to-do list, the next session, DM automation results, Instagram and page stats, club activity, and Ways to earn. New creators see a setup checklist instead.
- **Login with Instagram or Google:** one tap, with the profile filled in from Instagram.
- **Instagram-powered profile:** real posts, followers, engagement rate and insights on the Underdawg profile.
- **Schedule posts to Instagram:** plan posts and they go live on Instagram at the chosen time.
- **Link-in-bio page:** one link for the Instagram bio, with links, shop, bookings, tips and more. Fully editable.
- **Templates:** basic designs are free; premium designs come with the Pro plan.
- **Instagram DM automation:** the creator picks any word. When someone comments or DMs it, they automatically get a message, link, file, booking or payment.
- **Art Shop:** artists sell any art they make, such as original paintings, prints and handmade pieces, and ship it themselves. A print partner is optional for prints and products. AI mockups show the art on a wall or a product.
- **Live Rooms:** paid or free live sessions (e.g. a 2-hour dance class). Fans pay and join, and the room closes when the session ends.
- **Fan Club:** each creator's own community inside the app, Reddit-style. It has free and paid tiers, posts, upvotes, comments, polls, ratings and reviews, fan badges, and members-only perks.
- **Show & Review:** fans post their work (a drawing, a dance video, a cover), and the artist rates it and says what to fix. Help posts get marked "Solved". Paid members are reviewed first, and others can pay per review.
- **Artist Boards:** artist-only boards inside the app for brand reviews, scam alerts, rate checks and feedback.
- **Tips:** fans tip with UPI in one tap.
- **Digital downloads:** give away free assets, or sell presets, brushes, beats and wallpapers.
- **Bookings and 1:1 sessions:** fans pick a slot, pay a deposit and get a meeting link.
- **Event tickets:** sell tickets for shows and workshops.
- **Hire Artists (optional, for brands):** artists can switch on "Open for work". Brands search artists and hire from their profile or send a brief. The brand pays first, and the artist gets paid when the work is approved.
- **Gigs:** an open board where brands, venues, event organisers, cafés, colleges and individuals post paid gigs (performances, live art, freelance work). Artists apply with their portfolio and a quote, and payment is held until the gig is done.
- **Template marketplace:** designers sell their own page templates, and we take a cut.
- **Pro plan (₹299/month):** unlimited DM automations, premium templates, full stats, no Underdawg badge, custom domain.
