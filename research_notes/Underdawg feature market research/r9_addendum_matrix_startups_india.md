### Five most important 2024–2026 launches that overlap Underdawg
1. **Meta One creator/business plans (15 Sep 2026):** a paid Pro plan with 30-day Story scheduling, exportable analytics, audience insights, team access, a verified badge and auto follow-invites.
2. **Instagram creator marketplace in India (21 Feb 2024):** brand search on audience data, project briefs and a brand-deal inbox. It overlaps Hire Artists, Insights and Gigs in India.
3. **Edits (22 Apr 2025) and the India Edits Film Festival (24 Aug–8 Oct 2026):** jury-reviewed submissions, the closest big-platform analogue to Show & Review.
4. **YouTube Creator Partnerships with first-party insights sharing (India-eligible):** a partnerships hub announced in the 2026 CEO letter. https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/the-future-of-youtube-2026/ (21 Jan 2026)
5. **Patreon build-out:** Autopilot (Sep 2024), gift memberships (Nov 2024), native Live (Apr 2025), Niches and Live Q&As (Aug 2026).

**White space:** no big platform has native paid Show & Review, vetted artist-only boards or paid 1:1 booking. The nearest move is Meta's Forum app for Facebook Groups (US only, 29 Sep 2026). https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/find-community-forum-dedicated-app-facebook-groups/

### Five closest outside India
1. **Artfol:**
   - Overlaps: artists-only network, portfolios, commissions, marketplace, challenges and payment cards; half a million artists.
   - Missing: no Instagram-verified stats, DM automation, fan-club tiers, brand escrow or rate/scam boards were seen.
2. **Cara:**
   - Overlaps: artists-only, portfolio-led discovery and an anti-AI stance; 650K users in a week.
   - Missing: volunteer-run, no VC, no monetisation bundle.
3. **Beacons:**
   - Overlaps: link-in-bio, store, email, media kit and Beacons for Brands (Apr 2025); 7M+ creators.
   - Missing: serves all creators, with no artist community, critiques, boards or escrow.
4. **Planoly Creator Store (ex-Snipfeed):**
   - Overlaps: the closest tool bundle, with scheduling, automated DMs, link-in-bio store, print-on-demand via Printful, 1:1 bookings and memberships.
   - Missing: no artist community, discovery or gigs.
5. **Passes:**
   - Overlaps: memberships, paid DMs, 1:1 calls, livestreams and merch at a 10% fee.
   - Missing: skews to celebrities, and a Feb 2025 lawsuit shows trust-and-safety risk.

### Five closest in India
1. **Topmate:**
   - Overlaps: about six Underdawg money modules in one Indian product (1:1 bookings, webinars, downloads, Priority DM, Instagram Auto DM).
   - Differences: serves everyone, charges commission (10%/20%) instead of a subscription, and has no artist discovery, verified stats, print-on-demand, gigs, brand escrow or peer boards.
2. **StarClinch:**
   - Overlaps: artist-only profiles, a jobs board, client reviews and escrow-style payments, effectively Hire Artists, Gigs and Bookings combined.
   - Differences: a 15% fee, a ₹7.5 lakh penalty for off-platform deals, and no fan or Instagram tools.
3. **Talentrack:**
   - Overlaps: talent profiles, casting and a brand content marketplace with 50K+ talent.
   - Differences: a managed agency model leaning towards film and ad casting.
4. **TagMango (with Cosmofeed and Exly):**
   - Overlaps: paid communities, live sessions, courses, downloads and creator collaborations.
   - Differences: built for coaches and educators. Cosmofeed's Trustpilot rating of 1.8/5 suggests a creator-trust gap.
5. **IndieFolio:**
   - Overlaps: brands hire illustrators, designers and animators from a 70,000+ pool.
   - Differences: a managed B2B service, not a community.

- **FrontRow** (Bengaluru, 2020; celebrity-led hobby classes; $17.2M raised):
  - Fired nearly 90% of staff in 2022 (Entrackr, 14 Jun 2023).
  - Shut in 2023, per its Medium post of 13 Sep 2023.
- **Koo:**
  - Shut 3 Jul 2024, citing "an unpredictable market, failed partnerships and the high cost of technology services".
- **Qoohoo** (creator-fan engagement):
  - The domain is now parked; FY25 revenue ₹5.3 lakh, down 95.5% [Reported].
- **Unlu/Unluclass** (celebrity-led classes):
  - The domains are parked or do not resolve.
- **Leher** (live audio and video rooms):
  - leher.ai does not resolve; FY25 revenue ₹1.5K [Reported].
- **Trell:**
  - Revenue fell 94% to Rs 5 Cr in FY23 (Entrackr, 6 Mar 2024), after laying off about half its staff (Mar 2022).
- **Chingari:**
  - Operating revenue fell 53% in FY25 (Entrackr, 4 Mar 2026).
- **Gigstart** (Mumbai, 2013; artist booking):
  - No response from its site; last revenue ₹11.7 lakh in FY16 [Reported].
- **Rigi:**
  - Raised Rs 100 Cr (25 Jan 2023) for paid WhatsApp and Telegram creator communities.
  - Pivoted to "Rigi TV" short dramas.
- **One Impression** ($11M raised):
  - Its domain now redirects to an AI-video tool.
- **Eloelo:**
  - Now titled "EloTV | India's Drama App".
- **Hungama Artist Aloud** (indie music, 2012):
  - Expired TLS certificate, so likely neglected.
- **Shrinking:**
  - Mojarto (FY25 revenue down 62%) and Wobb (FY25 revenue down 72.4%).

### White space in India [Inference]
- **No combined player:** no Indian company combines artist-only discovery, verified Instagram stats, brand hiring with escrow and artist peer boards (brand reviews, scam alerts, rate checks).
- **The pieces sit with different companies:**
  - Topmate: money tools.
  - StarClinch: escrow bookings.
  - Talentrack and IndieFolio: brand hiring.
  - TagMango and Cosmofeed: paid communities.
  - Zorcha and InstantDM: DM automation.
  - BlinkStore and Qikink: print-on-demand.
- **Pricing:** no flat-fee artist plan like the ₹299/month Pro was found. Incumbents take commission (Topmate 10–20%, StarClinch 15%).
