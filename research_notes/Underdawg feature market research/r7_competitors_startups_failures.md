## 1. Direct competitors (brief section 16): which companies are closest to Underdawg?

### Takeaway
No one ships Underdawg's exact combination: artists only, Instagram-connected verified profile, fan-monetisation stack, artist-only trust boards, and brand hiring with escrow, launched India-first. But almost every individual layer is already sold by a stronger player.
- **Creator-business layer** (Instagram-connected page, comment-to-DM automation, scheduling, bookings, digital products, brand-deal profile): Linktree (70M+ users), Beacons and Stan already do this. Linktree's monetisation and brand-deal features are not available in India.
- **Artist-network layer:** Behance (65M+ members), DeviantArt (~110M registered), ArtStation, Cara and Artfol.
- **Hiring/escrow layer:** Behance, Dribbble, Contra; Fastwork in Southeast Asia; StarClinch in India.
- **Indian payments layer** (UPI storefronts and DM automation): Topmate, Cosmofeed, Exly, TagMango, Zorcha and LinkDM.

The closest analogues found are Linktree (bundle), Artfol (artist-only plus monetisation), Cohart (business tools for artists), and Behance/Dribbble/Contra (hiring with payments).

#### 1.1 Group A: "business side of Instagram" creator tools (functionally the closest to the current Underdawg direction)

**Linktree** (Australia/USA; https://linktr.ee; founded 2016)
- **Scale and offices:** "70M+ users worldwide"; HQ Melbourne, offices in Sydney, San Francisco and Los Angeles [Self-reported] ([Linktree About](https://linktr.ee/s/about), accessed 2026-10-01).
- **Target audience:** all creators and businesses (not artist-specific).
- **Core proposition:** one link for everything; it now also covers social scheduling, auto-posting, monetisation and analytics ([Linktree About](https://linktr.ee/s/about)).
- **Major features (each verified in Linktree's help centre):**
  - Instagram auto-reply. "Send your Linktree links via automatic direct message when someone comments on your IG post or Reel", triggered by any comment or by keywords. The Instagram account must be public. Monthly DM caps: Free 1,000 / Starter 1,500 / Pro 2,500 / Premium unlimited ([help article](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/9758234-use-instagram-auto-reply-to-automatically-share-links-with-your-audience)).
  - Social Planner ("Plann by Linktree"). "auto-posting (and cross-posting!) to all major platforms, including Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest and YouTube" (updated 2026-01-06) ([help](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/9885339-plan-and-schedule-social-media-posts-using-social-planner)); blog post "Here's how to schedule Instagram posts and save time" (2026-09-03) ([Linktree blog](https://linktr.ee/blog)).
  - Instagram grid. "connect and authorize Linktree to access your Instagram account", with "up to 5 links per post" (updated 2026-04-13) ([help](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/10143051-replicate-your-instagram-grid-on-linktree-with-clickable-posts)).
  - Bookings. "free or paid bookings directly from your Linktree", with Google Calendar and Google Meet ([help](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/11567751-how-to-offer-bookings-on-your-linktree)).
  - Digital products and courses (courses "powered by Kajabi"); Shops (affiliate/commissioned links); sponsored links ([fees help](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/11410206-understanding-transaction-and-processing-fees-on-linktree)).
  - "Selling Merch: A Guide for Creators" (help 8619687).
  - Brand Deals profile. "Creators and brands can now connect directly through the Brand Deals tab". After a creator connects an Instagram Professional or TikTok for Business account, "brands [can] view your performance metrics like engagement rates and audience reach"; rate cards are optional. Marked "US-only feature" (updated 2025-09-02) ([help](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/12135302-create-your-brand-deals-profile)).

  - Stripe processing: "2.9% + $0.30 per purchase" on digital products and courses ([help](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/11410206-understanding-transaction-and-processing-fees-on-linktree)).

#### 1.2 Group B: artist portfolio and community networks (global)

**Behance** (USA, Adobe; https://www.behance.net)
- **Launch and ownership:** launched 2005 per Wikipedia vs "Founded in 2006" per its About page (conflict) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behance); [About](https://www.behance.net/about)). Adobe bought it for "slightly more than $150 million" ([TechCrunch, 2012-12-21](https://techcrunch.com/2012/12/21/adobe-acquired-portfolio-service-behance-for-more-than-150-million-in-cash-and-stock/)).
- **Users:**
  - "over 65 million artists around the world" [Self-reported] ([Behance Year in Review 2025, 2025-12-18](https://www.behance.net/blog/year-in-review-2025)).
  - Recruiters can "Search 8M+ creative portfolios" ([Recruiter Pro, 2026-06-09](https://help.behance.net/hc/en-us/articles/51497046899227-Behance-Recruiter-Pro-Overview)).
  - Hiring volume: "$80M in job opportunities through more than 100,000 freelance projects, full-time jobs, and Adobe commissions" in 2025 ([Year in Review](https://www.behance.net/blog/year-in-review-2025)).
- **Target:** professional designers, illustrators, 3D artists, photographers, and hirers.
- **Major features:**
  - Portfolios; freelance services with payments; job board; Assets marketplace ("10,000+ Assets").
  - Pro: 0% fees, Boost, analytics, scheduled publishing on Behance ([Pro](https://www.behance.net/pro)).
  - Recruiter Pro "$399USD/month"; Adobe Creative Apprenticeship subsidies.
  - Livestreaming shut: "Starting December 16th, 2024, we will be discontinuing the ability to livestream directly on Behance" ([help](https://help.behance.net/hc/en-us/articles/30260603621787-Learn-More-Sunsetting-Livestreaming-on-Behance)).
  - Paid fan subscriptions launched Oct 2021 ([TechCrunch, 2021-10-26](https://techcrunch.com/2021/10/26/adobes-behance-adds-support-for-nfts-and-paid-subscriptions/)); current status unclear.
- **Discovery:**
  - Curated galleries: "Anyone can be featured in our galleries solely based on the quality of the work" ([Pro FAQ](https://help.behance.net/hc/en-us/articles/23707936129947-Behance-Pro-FAQ)).
  - Paid Boost.
  - "Priority placement on our Hire page" for available freelancers.
- **Monetisation:**
  - Pro "starting at $9.99/month"; shown in India as "₹797.68/mo".
  - Free-tier transactions "15-30% platform fees". The fee FAQ gives a sliding client/freelancer fee instead, so the two descriptions conflict ([fees FAQ, 2025-03-19](https://help.behance.net/hc/en-us/articles/10770324288923-FAQ-What-are-the-fees)).
  - Assets: 30% fee for non-Pro sellers.
- **India:** payouts via PayPal. India is among countries where Stripe is "supported generally, but do not support the collection of a platform fee" ([country FAQ](https://help.behance.net/hc/en-us/articles/13824610641179-FAQ-Why-isn-t-my-country-supported)).
- **Strengths [Inference]:** Adobe distribution; hiring and payments in one place; INR pricing.
- **Weaknesses [Inference]:**
  - Designer/agency skew; high free-tier fees.
  - No fan monetisation; no Instagram layer.
  - Its live product failed.
- **Artist complaints:** none verified as recurring.
- **Learn [Inference]:**
  - Fees that drop to 0% with a subscription.
  - A hirer subscription as a second revenue line.
  - An "available for work" flag that feeds hirer search.
- **Do NOT copy [Inference]:** a 15-30% take on emerging artists; launching live features without a sustaining audience.

### Inferences (Section 1)
- **The functional threat is incumbents, not artist networks.** For the current product direction, the closest competitors are creator-business toolkits (Linktree, Beacons, Stan; Topmate, Exly, TagMango, Cosmofeed, Zorcha and LinkDM in India). Artist networks (Behance, Cara, DeviantArt) matter less. The toolkits are free or cheap, well funded, and ship Instagram-native automation quickly.
- **Artist-only networks win attention during trust shocks but struggle to pay artists or themselves.**
  - Cara's surge came from Meta's AI policy; it runs on volunteers and donations.
  - Artfol is small.
  - Behance and DeviantArt make money through parent ecosystems and fees.
- **The demand side pays more reliably than artists do.** Behance Recruiter Pro charges $399/month, VSCO Hub $19.99/month, ArtConnect $99 per post and Dribbble $150/month per job. Talentrack and Kofluence monetise brands. This supports charging brands for Hire Artists rather than relying on a Rs 299 artist Pro plan.
- **Escrow and price-floor protection are the norm wherever artists sell work to strangers:**
  - Commission platforms (Dribbble, Contra, Skeb, Mihuashi, Crepe, Fastwork) all hold the money.
  - Skeb's average commission rose from about ¥11,000 to about ¥14,000 once it hid price search.
- **Show & Review has real precedents:** Skeb "Advice", Stage 32 coverage at $49, Groover's €2 pitches with 4M+ reviews, Notefolio portfolio feedback, ADPList (36,000+ mentors) and Topmate Priority DM.
- **Artist Boards appear unoccupied inside any platform.** The nearest analogues are external: FYPM, Writer Beware, Artists-Beware.

## 2. Indirect competitors - who competes for the artist's time, content, portfolio, audience, community and opportunities (brief section 17)

### Takeaway
Underdawg's real competitor for an Indian artist's attention is Instagram itself (481M Indian ad-reach users, +22.9% year on year), which already gives artists a free portfolio grid, reach, native scheduling, subscriptions, gifts and a brand-partnership marketplace. Linktree, YouTube, WhatsApp/Telegram, Discord, Patreon, Etsy, Fiverr and Spotify each take a slice of the artist's time, money or opportunities. Because Underdawg has no feed of its own, it does not compete for content or attention directly. It competes for the business layer on top (links, DMs, payments, bookings, brand work), and that layer is the one Instagram and Linktree are moving fastest into.

### Inferences (Section 2)
- **Instagram is both Underdawg's host and its biggest competitor.** It already lists subscriptions, gifts, badges, bonuses, a creator marketplace and partnership ads for creators. ## 3. Global startup search, roughly 2021-2026 (brief section 18)

### Takeaway
Between 2021 and 2026, new entrants have clustered into four camps:
- **Anti-AI or artist-only social networks:** Cara, Artfol, Layers. They grow in backlash spikes, but none has a working revenue model for artists at scale.
- **Creator-business toolkits:** Linktree's expansion, Stan, Beacons, Laylo, Mainstack, and in India Topmate, Exly, TagMango, Zorcha and LinkDM. Well funded and crowded.
- **Hiring and escrow marketplaces for creative work:** Contra, Dribbble's 2025 pivot, Cohart, NOVA, Fastwork, Skeb, Crepe, Mihuashi/Huajia.
- **Superfan and fan-club platforms:** EVEN, Weverse, b.stage, Fanicon, Xfolio, FANBOX.

No startup found combines all four for artists, India-first. Because web search was unavailable, small or stealth startups (especially Indian seed-stage ones) may be missing.

### Inferences (Section 3)
- **Startup activity, 2021-2026:** it moved away from "yet another artist social network" (Creatively is likely defunct, Polywork and Read.cv/Posts shut, Layers and Artfol are small). It moved toward:
  - commerce and hiring rails: Contra, Cohart, Dribbble's escrow pivot, NOVA;

  - Instagram automation: Laylo, Zorcha, LinkDM, Linktree.

## 4. Failed, shut-down, pivoted or faded products - and why (brief section 19)

### Takeaway
Artist and creator networks rarely die from lack of artists; they die from (1) no durable reason to return once the launch hype fades (Ello, Peach, Clubhouse, BeReal, Vero), (2) creators not getting paid, or the platform losing money while creators are owed (Vine, Talenthouse, Paddle8, Byte, Koo), (3) losing a parent or acquirer's support (Ello under Talenthouse, Bandcamp under Epic and then Songtradr, Koji and Bento under Linktree, Read.cv/Posts under Perplexity, IGTV, Spotify Live, Bulletin, Revue), and (4) dependence on another company's platform or API (Twitter clients in 2023, Instagram Basic Display API apps in Dec 2024). The last point applies directly to Underdawg, whose core profile is built on Meta's Instagram API.

#### 4.2 The most instructive cases for an artists-only network (short narratives)

**1. Talenthouse → Ello → EyeEm: a creative roll-up that stopped paying artists**
- **What happened [Verified via Wikipedia and its Guardian citations]:**
  - Talenthouse sold "paid creative opportunities": brand briefs that artists answered.
  - It bought Ello in 2018. It then rolled Ello, Zooppa, EyeEm and Jovoto into a holding company that listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange in March 2022.
  - February 2023: creatives said they had not been paid for completed work, and the co-founder apologised for an "unacceptable delay in payments". April 2023: the Guardian reported the company was "close to failure as debts mount".
  - Ello went offline in July 2023 ([Wikipedia: Talenthouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talenthouse); [Wikipedia: Ello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ello_(social_network))).
  - EyeEm went bankrupt (April 2023) and was sold to Freepik. In April 2024 its new terms asked photographers to grant rights for "training ... machine-learning models". It closed on 13 January 2026 ([Wikipedia: EyeEm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EyeEm)).
- **Lesson for Underdawg:** "Hire Artists" and "Gigs" are only credible if brand money sits in escrow before work starts. Payouts must never depend on Underdawg's own cash position. Artists must be able to export their work, and their work must never be relicensed for AI training.

**2. Vine: creators leave when they can't earn, and the incumbent copies**
- **What happened [Verified]:**
  - Vine had more than 200M active users (December 2015).
  - Between January and June 2016, "more than half" of users with more than 15,000 followers stopped posting or left for YouTube, Instagram and Snapchat ([Business Insider via Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vine_(service))).
  - Instagram had already added 15-second video. Twitter shut Vine on 17 January 2017, partly because "marketers" left.
- **Lesson for Underdawg:** the artists who bring audiences need a reason to stay that Instagram does not give them, namely income or opportunities. Any successful mechanic that Underdawg invents can be copied by Instagram within months.

**3. The live-audio and live-video bust (Clubhouse, Spotify Live, IGTV, Behance Live)**
- **Clubhouse** went from 8.1M downloads (February 2021) to a $4B valuation (April 2021). Its users then "declined 21% during three weeks" while Twitter, Facebook, Spotify, Discord and Reddit all copied it. It cut 50% of staff in April 2023 ([Wikipedia: Clubhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clubhouse_(app))).
- **Spotify Live** was built on a $50M acquisition and closed on 30 April 2023 ([Wikipedia: Spotify Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotify_Live)).
- **IGTV** had about 7M users by January 2020; its app was killed in March 2022 ([Wikipedia: IGTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IGTV)).
- **Behance** ended livestreaming on 16 December 2024 ([Behance help](https://help.behance.net/hc/en-us/articles/30260603621787-Learn-More-Sunsetting-Livestreaming-on-Behance)).
- **Lesson for Underdawg:** Live Rooms should be scheduled, ticketed sessions (classes, workshops, critiques) that close when they end, not an always-on social space. Expect Instagram and YouTube Live to remain the default for free live.

**4. Koo and India's post-TikTok short-video wave: India-first is not a moat**
- **Koo** raised $30M from Tiger Global (May 2021) and reached about a $275M valuation (November 2022). It peaked at 2.1M daily and 10M monthly users, against a claimed 60M "users". It halted salaries in April 2024 and shut on 3 July 2024, blaming "failed partnership talks and high technology costs" ([Business Today, 2024-07-03](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/little-yellow-bird-says-final-goodbye-indias-twitter-rival-koo-shuts-down-435555-2024-07-03)).
- **The short-video wave:** ShareChat paid "$700 million" for MX TakaTak and then laid off about 20% (January 2023) and about 15% (December 2023) of staff ([Wikipedia: ShareChat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ShareChat)).
- **Instagram's growth over the same period:** Instagram's ad reach in India reached 481M, up 22.9% year on year ([DataReportal, 2025-11-05](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-india)).
- **Lesson for Underdawg:** Indian creators follow reach. Underdawg is right to complement Instagram rather than compete with it as a feed. It must also stay capital-light and earn revenue early.

**5. Myspace, PureVolume and 8tracks: the artist's archive is the asset**
- **Myspace** lost "over 50 million songs" and "12 years' worth of content" in a 2019 server migration ([Wikipedia: Myspace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myspace)).
- **PureVolume**, a discovery site for emerging bands, closed on 30 June 2018, and users had to save their own music ([Wikipedia: PureVolume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PureVolume)).
- **8tracks** fell from 8M monthly users (2014) to under 1M and closed under royalty debts (31 December 2019) ([Wikipedia: 8tracks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8tracks)).
- **Lesson for Underdawg:** guarantee backups and one-click export. Do not host third-party rights-bearing content (music, brand IP) without a licensing model.

**6. Link-in-bio and creative-profile consolidation (Koji, Bento, Read.cv/Posts, Polywork)**
- Koji was bought by Linktree (2021, single source), and withkoji.com is unreachable. bento.me now redirects to linktr.ee [Observed 2026-10-01].
- Read.cv was acquired by Perplexity, began winding down on 17 January 2025 and allowed export until 16 May ([TechCrunch, 2025-01-17](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/17/perplexity-acquires-read-cv-a-social-media-platform-for-professionals/)). posts.cv returns "DEPLOYMENT_DISABLED" [Observed by sub-researcher 2026-10-01].
- polywork.com no longer resolves [Observed 2026-10-01].
- **Lesson for Underdawg:** a beautifully designed profile or link page is not a defensible business. Design-led creative profile networks have been bought and shut, or have quietly died, within a few years.

**7. Platform dependence (Twitter 2023, Instagram 2024): the existential risk for an Instagram-connected product**
- **Twitter, 2023:** Twitter deliberately cut off third-party clients on 12 January 2023; Tweetbot was pulled on 19 January ([Wikipedia: Tweetbot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tweetbot)).
- **Instagram, 2024:** Meta ended the Instagram Basic Display API on 4 December 2024. Only professional (business or creator) accounts can now connect ([Meta, 2024-09-04](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2024/09/04/update-on-instagram-basic-display-api/); [Meta Instagram Platform overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/overview)). Comment-to-DM allows "Only one message ... to the commenter" within 7 days, and follow-ups are allowed only within 24 hours of a reply ([Meta Private Replies](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/private-replies)).
- **Already causing 1-star reviews of Instagram-dependent creator tools in 2026:**
  - Stan: "Auto DMs do not work and no human can help"
  - Beacons: "trying to connect my social accounts ... repeatedly froze or failed"
  - Hoopr: "You cannot connect your insta"
  - Sources: [Stan](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6478343472), [Beacons](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6444589346), [Hoopr India](https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=hoopr&entity=software&country=in).
- **Disabled Instagram accounts:** Indian Instagram users posted several 1-star reviews on a single day (29 September 2026) saying their accounts had been disabled for "Account Integrity" ([App Store India](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/id389801252)). When that happens, an Instagram-based Underdawg profile breaks.
- **Lesson for Underdawg:**
  - Require professional accounts at onboarding.
  - Cache the artist's own portfolio so the page survives an Instagram outage or ban.
  - Collect fan contacts (email, phone, WhatsApp opt-in) so the audience relationship lives on Underdawg.
  - Never make Instagram API access the only source of value.

**8. Policy shocks: Tumblr's adult-content ban and DeviantArt's DreamUp**
- **Tumblr** "lost 30% of its user traffic" after its December 2018 adult-content ban and was later sold for reportedly "less than $3 million" (bought for $1.1B) ([Wikipedia: Tumblr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumblr)).
- **DeviantArt** opted everyone's art into AI training by default in November 2022 and reversed it within hours after backlash ([Ars Technica, 2022-11-11](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2022/11/deviantart-upsets-artists-with-its-new-ai-art-generator-dreamup/)).
- **Lesson for Underdawg:** publish stable content, AI and data policies before scale. Artists move fast when trust breaks; Cara is the other side of that move ([TechCrunch, 2024-06-06](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/06/a-social-app-for-creatives-cara-grew-from-40k-to-650k-users-in-a-week-because-artists-are-fed-up-with-metas-ai-policies/)).

#### 4.3 Cross-case lessons for Underdawg
1. **Payout trust beats feature count.**
   - Talenthouse and Paddle8 failed artists on money.
   - Indian creator storefronts already attract "withdrawal" and "fraud" 1-star reviews (Cosmofeed, 3 reviews, [App Store India](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/id1592830857)).
   - So: escrow for Hire Artists, ring-fenced client money, published payout timelines (as Saatchi does: "up to 19 days"), and KYC done up front, not at withdrawal.
2. **Never take AI-training rights. Make NoAI the default** (EyeEm, DeviantArt, ArtStation's 2026 reversal, and Cara's growth).
3. **Data portability and durability** (Myspace, PureVolume, Ello, Read.cv): export plus backups, and a public pledge of continuity if the company is sold.
4. **Expect acquisition and ownership churn even at trusted platforms** (Bandcamp → Songtradr, ArtStation → KitBash, Behance and DeviantArt under parents). Being independent is itself a positioning asset.
5. **Earning has to come early, without charging beginners first.**
   - Creators leave platforms where they can't earn (Vine, Byte).
   - Beginners resent paywalls (Stan trial complaints, DeviantArt Core required to sell, Glass paid posting, Singulart subscription plus 50%).
   - Charge the demand side (brands, hirers, organisations) or take a small cut of success.
6. **Novelty is not retention** (Ello's "20% ... active ... one week after", Peach, BeReal, Clubhouse). Measure week-4 artist retention and real outcomes such as bookings, gigs and paid deals, not sign-ups.
7. **Instagram copies fast** (Vine → 15-second video; BeReal → Dual; Clubhouse → Spaces etc.). Build what Instagram structurally won't: artist-only trust (brand reviews, rate data), escrowed small-brand hiring, India-native payments.
8. **Live is spiky and expensive** (Clubhouse, Spotify Live, IGTV, Behance Live). Keep Live Rooms as paid, scheduled sessions.
9. **Platform dependence is Underdawg's single biggest structural risk** (Twitter 2023; Instagram Basic Display API 2024; Instagram account disables).
10. **Vanity metrics mislead** (Google+'s 540M "MAU" with sessions under 5 seconds; Koo's 60M "users" against 10M MAU).
11. **A discovery-only product for unknowns rarely funds itself** (PureVolume; Cara depends on donations).
    - Pair discovery with a paying demand side: Behance Recruiter Pro at $399/month, VSCO Hub at $19.99/month for hirers, ArtConnect at $99 per opportunity post, Dribbble job listings at $150/month.
12. **Pay-to-play visibility erodes artist trust** (Saatchi Sponsored Listings 2026, Dribbble pay-to-rank and its 2025 bans, Stage 32 pay-to-pitch, Sonicbids paid submissions). Keep discovery merit-based and visibly fair to unknowns.
13. **Surges arrive suddenly and cost real money** (Cara's $96,280 weekly bill; Hive Social's security shutdown). Set spending caps and pass security reviews before any marketing push.
14. **India-specific:**
    - Capital-intensive social products die without a revenue model (Koo).
    - Short-video clones won MAU but lost the creator economy to Reels.
    - Global link-in-bio monetisation does not work in India: Linktree's earning features exclude India, and Stan lacks Stripe India. That gap is real, but Indian UPI storefronts (Topmate, Exly, TagMango, Cosmofeed) already fill it.

### Inferences (Section 4)
- **Main failure modes, in order of frequency:**
  1. Novelty without retention.
  2. Creators or payees not paid, or no creator income.
  3. Acquired-and-shut or parent distress.
  4. Platform or policy dependence.
- Every Underdawg pillar maps to at least one documented failure:
  - Live Rooms → Clubhouse, Spotify Live, Behance Live, pixiv Sketch Live.
  - Hire Artists / Gigs → Talenthouse, Sofar pay controversy, Sonicbids.
  - Instagram-connected profile and DM automation → Twitter API 2023, Instagram Basic Display API 2024.
  - Art Shop → Redbubble/Society6 pressure, Spring (unverified).
  - Artist-only community → Ello, Cara's volunteer fragility.

### Gaps (Section 4)
- **No source found:** Drip (Kickstarter), Coverfly, IRL, Lomotif, Dispo, Fanhouse, Superpeer, Frontrow/Unluclass/Tring/Trell/Chingari (India), Mojarto (site unreachable), Hipi.
- **Shutdown date and reasons not documented:** Polywork; Koji and Bento (acquisition dates single-source or unverified).
- **No numbers on unpaid amounts:** the Guardian Talenthouse articles could not be fetched; details come via Wikipedia.
- **Spring/Teespring:** the shutdown claim rests on an unsourced Wikipedia note.
- **Geneva:** the brief assumed a shutdown; evidence shows it is live under Bumble.

## 6. Is anyone building something extremely close to Underdawg?

### Takeaway
**Short answer:** no single company was found building the same thing: artists only, India-first, an Instagram-connected verified profile, a fan-monetisation stack, artist-only trust boards, and brand hiring with escrow. But the product is far less unique than the feature list suggests.
- **Instagram business layer:** about 60% of Underdawg's 18 features (the Instagram-connected business layer plus fan monetisation) already exist together in Linktree, Beacons and Stan globally, and in Topmate, Exly, TagMango, Cosmofeed, Zorcha and LinkDM in India.
- **Artist-network layer:** exists separately in Behance, DeviantArt, Artfol, Cara, pixiv and Xfolio.
- **Truly rare features:** only Artist Boards (brand reviews, scam alerts, rate checks), and to a lesser degree Show & Review and a page-template marketplace.

Treat this as a conclusion under constraints. Web search was unavailable, so small or stealth Indian startups may have been missed (see Gaps).

### Cited Findings

**(i) The same bundle (Instagram-connected link-in-bio, DM automation, monetisation, brand profile), not artist-specific**
- **Linktree** ([help centre](https://linktr.ee/help/en/collections/7058291-how-to-s)) already offers:
  - Instagram grid sync with clickable posts.
  - Instagram comment-to-DM auto-reply (1,000 DMs a month free).
  - Plann-powered scheduling and auto-posting to Instagram.
  - Free or paid bookings, digital products and Kajabi-powered courses.
  - A Brand Deals tab showing brands "engagement rates and audience reach" from a connected Instagram Professional account.
  - It claims "70M+ users worldwide" ([About](https://linktr.ee/s/about)).
  - **India gap:** earning features exclude India, and Brand Deals and Shops are US-only ([eligibility](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/11126119-who-can-use-linktree-s-earn-features); [Brand Deals](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/12135302-create-your-brand-deals-profile)).
- **Beacons** ([help](https://help.beacons.ai)): media kit from connected accounts, "Smart Reply Instagram DMs", store, email marketing and AI brand outreach.
- **Stan** ([help](https://help.stan.store)): AutoDM, store, coaching calls, memberships and community, plus a separate community app. No Stripe accounts for India ([help](https://help.stan.store/article/217-countries-available-for-stripe-custom-accounts)).
- **Laylo** ([laylo.com](https://laylo.com/)), for artists specifically: Instagram keyword → automatic drop notifications for tickets, merch and content.

**(ii) The same audience (artists only, or artist-first), without the Instagram business layer**
- **Artfol:** AI-free, artist-only social network with shop, commissions, tips, a pay link and brand partnerships; "half a million artists" [Self-reported] ([artfol.app](https://artfol.app)).
- **Cara:** anti-AI artist network with "over a million people" [Self-reported] and a jobs board, but no monetisation for artists ([Cara FAQ](https://blog.cara.app/faq)).
- **Behance:** "65 million" members; hiring, payments and INR Pro pricing ([Behance](https://www.behance.net/blog/year-in-review-2025)).
- **DeviantArt:** full fan-monetisation stack; "$23 million" creator sales in 2025 ([DeviantArt](https://www.deviantart.com/team/journal/DeviantArt-at-25-Why-deliberate-growth-matters-1295430763)).
- **Xfolio (Japan):** page templates, shop, downloads, tips and tiered fan clubs ([Xfolio](https://xfolio.jp/about)).
- **Cohart (USA):** "social marketplace" plus business tools for artists ([TechCrunch, 2023-08-02](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/02/cohart-art-marketplace-commerce/)).
- **BandLab (music):** Opportunities/gigs, Tip Jar, a paid Verified badge and Pro plans priced in rupees ([BandLab](https://blog.bandlab.com/bandlab-opportunities-picks/); [App Store India](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/bandlab-music-making-studio/id968585775)).

  - Topmate: "1 million+ professionals" for 1:1 sessions, webinars, products and Priority DM ([topmate.io](https://topmate.io)).

  - StarClinch: "India's Leading Artist Booking Platform" ([starclinch.com](https://starclinch.com)).
  - Talentrack: "50K+ artists, experts, and influencers" ([talentrack.in](https://www.talentrack.in)).

- **Ticketing:** District (Eternal), launched Nov 2024 ([Wikipedia: Eternal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eternal_Limited)).

### Inferences
- **The "business side of Instagram" bundle is a commodity.** Linktree, Beacons, Stan and the Indian tools offer most of it, often free; Zorcha advertises "unlimited free DM automations".
  - Underdawg's Rs 299 Pro plan, built on "unlimited DM automations, premium templates, full stats", competes on price with free Indian tools and on features with well-funded global ones.
  - Instagram itself keeps adding native creator tools (subscriptions, gifts, creator marketplace).
- **The bundle mainly helps artists who already have an audience.** DM automation, tips, fan clubs, tickets and downloads monetise existing followers; Weverse fans send ~138 times more messages than artists do ([Weverse 2024 report](https://en.weverse.co/news/?q=YToxOntzOjEyOiJrZXl3b3JkX3R5cGUiO3M6MzoiYWxsIjt9&bmode=view&idx=165565023&t=board)).
  - With "no separate in-app feed", the only discovery surfaces for an unknown artist are Instagram itself and brands searching Hire Artists.
  - That conflicts with the core objective in research.md: help under-discovered artists get seen.
  - **Risk:** Underdawg becomes "Linktree/Stan for Indian artists" (crowded, low moat) while the discovery promise goes undelivered.
- **The genuinely unoccupied territory:**
  - **An artists-only trust and opportunity layer:** brand reviews, scam alerts and rate floors. Only FYPM (influencers, US) and small volunteer groups exist.
  - **Escrowed small-brand hiring of Indian artists, priced in INR/UPI:** Behance pays India via PayPal; Linktree Brand Deals is US-only; Fiverr is criticised for "pushing down prices".
  - **Merit-based discovery for unknowns:** SoundCloud First Fans, pixiv rookie rankings and ZCOOL's curation tiers are the models.
  - These depend on network density (enough artists and brands), so they work only after a cold start.
- **What would make a competitor "extremely close" quickly:**
  - Linktree enabling Earn and Brand Deals in India.
  - BandLab extending Opportunities and Verified to visual and performing artists.
  - Behance adding Instagram import plus INR/UPI payouts.
  - Topmate or Kofluence adding an artist-only brand marketplace.
  - Each is a plausible near-term move by an incumbent with distribution.

### Gaps
- **Discovery limits:** no web search, and Crunchbase, Tracxn, Inc42/YourStory/Entrackr tag pages, Product Hunt and Reddit were blocked. Indian seed-stage artist apps and small "Kalakar"-type apps may be missing.
- **Naming conflicts:** whether another artist or creator platform uses the 'UnderDawg'/'Underdog' name was not verified; that pass did not return before handback.
- **Company details not obtained:** Superprofile and Cosmofeed (their sites blocked automated access); Topmate's funding; Beacons' and Stan's funding and user numbers; Manychat's figures (site blocked).
