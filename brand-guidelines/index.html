MANIFESTO · 04 / 62
UNDERDAWG
A. Foundation · Brand Story
The Story

A market broken
on both sides.

The Creator Side
10 million creators in India. 99% are invisible. Algorithms reward popularity, not potential. YouTube needs 1K subs + 4K hours. Instagram pays nothing. Spotify pays ₹0.003 per stream.
Average time to first ₹1 earned as a creator: 2+ years.
The Brand Side
Brands pay ₹50L for a celebrity post that converts at 0.3%. They can't find authentic mid-tier creators. They can't tell who's real and who's bot-padded. 60% say influencer ROI is their #1 problem.
Result: brands overpay. Creators underearn. The market loses on both sides.
What we saw
The middle — creators with 10K to 100K followers, 3–8% engagement, zero infrastructure — is the most engaged, most ignored segment in the creator economy. That's where the ROI lives. That's our wedge.

B. Verbal Identity · Name & Meaning
Etymology

Why "Underdawg"

The deliberate misspelling
A spelling that signals culture — hip-hop, internet-native, lowercase rebellion. Dawg is what you call your homie. The brand is for the dawgs before it's about them.
Under, not over
Not a brag. Not "Top Creator." We're for the ones still climbing. The day a creator becomes "over-dawg," they've graduated — and they didn't get there alone.
The thesis, in one word
Under
dawg.
Underdog — the contender. Boxing slang since the 1880s. The one not expected to win.
-awg — the culture. The dawg in your corner. The crew.

SECTION G · 43 / 62

G. Motion · Principles
Principles

Four rules.
No exceptions.

01
Quick — not jumpy.
Default duration is 220ms. Under 200ms feels twitchy. Over 350ms feels slow. We use one speed unless we have a reason not to.
02
Ease-out, almost always.
Elements arrive fast, settle gently. Ease-in (slow start) is reserved for elements leaving the screen.
03
No bounce. No spring.
No overshoot, no rubber-banding. The brand is gritty editorial, not playful SaaS. Motion respects the type.
04
Motion has a reason.
If it doesn't show state change, hierarchy, or relationship — don't animate it. Decoration is not a use case.
